# CrazyPoly — Deleting your account and your data

**CrazyPoly** is published on Google Play by **NeatHippo** — Dmytro Korchynskyi, an
individual based in Ukraine. This page explains how to have your CrazyPoly account and the
data attached to it deleted, what is deleted, and what is kept afterwards and for how long.
It is the short, practical version of §11 and §12 of our
[Privacy Policy](https://neathippo.com/privacy-policy/), which governs and which carries
our full contact and postal details.

**Contact for any request on this page: support@neathippo.com.**

**Most players have nothing to delete.** Playing alone, against the robots, or with people
on the same Wi-Fi needs no account and sends us nothing. An account exists only if you
signed in for online play, coins or a friends list.

---

## 1. Delete your account yourself, in the game

The fastest route, and it needs nothing from us. It takes effect immediately.

1. Open **CrazyPoly** on your device.
2. Tap the **player button** on the main menu — the round one with a person on it. It opens the screen titled **User Account**.
3. Sign in, if you are not signed in already — the account can only be deleted from inside it.
4. Tap **Delete account**.
5. Read the first confirmation, which says what is about to be lost, and confirm it.
6. Confirm the second one — **Delete for good**.

Your account and everything on our servers attached to it are then gone. There is no
waiting period, no request queue, and no way to undo it.

**One thing can block it:** an account with a **negative coin balance** cannot be deleted
until the balance is no longer negative — a debt is settled, not escaped. Everything else
deletes at any time.

## 2. Ask us to delete it, if you cannot reach that button

If you have lost the device, cannot sign in, or have uninstalled the game:

1. E-mail **support@neathippo.com** from **the e-mail address on the account**. For an
   account that has no e-mail address — the "this device only" kind — write from the
   device itself and tell us the **display name** you played under.
2. Say that you want your CrazyPoly account deleted.
3. We will confirm details only the account's owner would know, so that nobody else can
   have your account deleted, and then delete it.

We answer within **30 days**, and normally much sooner.

## 3. Delete some of your data without deleting your account

You do not have to close the account to have data removed. E-mail
**support@neathippo.com** and name what you want gone — your **statistics**, your
**friends list**, the **records of your finished matches** — and we will delete those and
leave the account in place. Your friends list you can also prune yourself, one name at a
time, in **Friends**: removing somebody deletes that friendship on our servers rather than
hiding it.

## What is deleted

Deleting the account deletes, from our servers:

* the **account record** — your display name, and the sign-in identifiers attached to it
  (your e-mail address, or the Google account identifier, or the device identifier);
* your **statistics** — games won, lost and abandoned;
* your **coin balance** and the record of which **themes** the account paid for;
* your **friends list**, the friend requests you sent or received, and your presence record;
* your **match records** — who was at the table and how the game went;
* any **e-mail confirmation or password-reset codes** still in flight.

## What is kept, and for how long

Four things are not removed by deleting the account, and we would rather say so here than
in a reply to a request:

| What | Why | How long |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Purchase records** — the product bought, Google's purchase token and receipt | Financial records we are required by law to keep, and the only way a purchase can be honoured or re-granted | As long as the law requires, which outlasts the account |
| **A starting-coins marker** — a note that this device has already been given the free coins a new account starts with | It stops a new account on the same device collecting them again; a marker that expired would only postpone the loophole it closes. It names a device, not you, and is stored as a one-way hash | For as long as we run the online service |
| **Anonymous usage counts** (Privacy Policy §6.10) | They carry a random identifier belonging to an *installation*, never to you or your account. There is nothing in them that points at a person, so there is nothing for us to find and remove | Kept as counts, for as long as they are useful for judging a release |
| **Server logs** | Operational and security records, written as the service runs | A short operational window, normally no more than 90 days |

Two more things are worth knowing, because they look like exceptions and are not:

* **Advertising data is not ours to delete.** Ads on Android are served by Google AdMob,
  which collects what it collects as an independent controller under
  [Google's own privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and its own retention
  rules. Buying **Remove Ads** stops the requests; deleting your CrazyPoly account does not
  reach into Google's records, because they were never ours.
* **What is on your own device is deleted by uninstalling.** Settings, an unfinished game,
  local statistics, your sign-in token and the game's log file live in the app's private
  storage and never leave it unless you send us a log yourself. **Uninstalling CrazyPoly
  removes all of it.** A purchase made through Google Play stays on your Google account and
  can be restored — that is Google's record, not ours.
